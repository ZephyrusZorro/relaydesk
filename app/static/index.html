Built for an inspectable demo.

RelayDesk is an original IT support capstone. n8n coordinates three bounded specialist roles: classifier, resolver and reviewer. Native LangChain nodes connect them to a local Ollama model.

The data service provides a LangChain BM25 retriever and SQLite persistence. The included procedures are fictional. A source citation and model review do not prove a response is correct; the operator reviews the final answer.

The portal runs as a local single-user workspace. Optional mailbox intake requires configuration. Speech availability depends on your browser and OS.
