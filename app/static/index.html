Built for an inspectable demo.

RelayDesk 2.0 is an original IT support capstone orchestrated in n8n. Requests enter a durable SQLite job queue, are dispatched by an independent worker with lease recovery, and flow through three native n8n specialist agents (classifier, resolver and reviewer) connected to a local Ollama model.

The data service provides a versioned, cached LangChain hybrid lexical retriever (BM25 + character 3-gram similarity), authenticated n8n connection verification, and atomic approval/outbox persistence. The included procedures are fictional; operator review remains mandatory.

The portal runs as a local single-user workspace. Optional mailbox intake requires configuration. Speech availability depends on your browser and OS.
